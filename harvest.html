India's Spice Harvest Seasons

India's diverse climate and fertile soil create ideal conditions for growing premium spices. Understanding harvest seasons helps buyers plan procurement and secure fresh, high-quality spices at the right time.

01

Turmeric

Premium Indian turmeric is generally harvested after the crop reaches maturity and the leaves begin to dry.

Harvest: January – March
02

Coriander

Coriander seeds are harvested when the plants mature and the seeds develop their characteristic aroma.

Harvest: February – April
03

Red Chilli

Red chillies are harvested after the pods mature and develop the desired colour, heat and flavour.

Harvest: February – April
04

Cumin

Cumin is harvested when the plants dry and the seeds reach the required maturity and aroma.

Harvest: February – April
